How this tool works

This tool is where your whole submission is built. Work the simulator panels below, then answer every numbered question from the handout in the submission panel at the bottom — the numbering there matches the handout exactly. Your work is saved in this browser as you type. When everything is answered, press Build submission PDF and save the file it prints; that single PDF is what you upload to Canvas.

The simulator keeps its own record. Press Build submission sheet when the panels are complete — the sheet it assembles is harvested into your PDF automatically.

Panel 2 — the hardware cards

Card C1

2U chassis, twelve large hot-swap drive sleds across the front in three rows, two PSU bays and a management RJ45 marked with a wrench icon at the rear.

Card C2

Heavy 2U unit with a small LCD status screen, one row of C13 outlets on the rear, a large battery tray behind the front bezel, cooling vents along both sides.

Card C3

A bare lesson the size of a hardback book: a fan at one end, an IEC power inlet at the other, a card-edge connector down one side, a plastic pull handle.

Card C4

1U chassis, 24 RJ45 ports with green/amber link LEDs above each, two SFP cages, a power switch and inlet at the rear.

Card C5

A flat sealed rectangle in a 2.5-inch form factor, no moving parts audible, SATA connectors, label carrying a serial number, capacity in GB and the word 'Solid State'.

Card C6

A long flat boxed pair of sliding metal channels with ball-bearing runners, bags of cage nuts and screws taped inside the box lid.
